Our method

Every habit is given an honest rating

Tradition is not the same as proof, and proof is not the same as hype. Each habit we cover is placed on one of three levels, and we show the source.

Well supported

Good studies or reviews back the specific claim we make.

Some evidence

Small trials or observational studies. We say exactly how strong.

Tradition, not yet proven

Valued in daily life, but research has not confirmed the claims. We say so.
