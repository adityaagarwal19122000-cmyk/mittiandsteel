Why Mitti & Steel

We are two friends from Kolkata who grew up with clay pots, steel plates, ghee and early mornings, and who wanted to know which of those old habits actually hold up.

Mitti is clay, the earth under the pot. Steel is the thali on almost every Indian table. Both are ordinary, everyday objects, and that is the point: small habits, not expensive fixes.

Tradition deserves respect. It also deserves a fair check.

How we check

  1. We separate what the tradition says, what studies show, and what is unproven.
  2. We prefer reviews and randomised trials, and we say when a study is small or observational.
  3. We link the source for every health claim.
  4. We never say "cures", "detoxes" or "heals".
  5. If a habit does not hold up, we say so.

We are not doctors. Nothing here is medical advice.
